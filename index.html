Focus

I'm an Electronic & Telecommunication Engineering undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa, focused on digital IC design and verification. I write synthesizable SystemVerilog, from arithmetic IP and a RISC-V CPU to a CNN accelerator, and build self-checking testbenches to verify it.

I'm currently an Engineering Intern in Hardware Acceleration at LSEG. Alongside the digital work I'm learning analog IC design with Cadence and the open-source IIC-OSIC-TOOLS flow. I started out designing multi-layer PCBs, so I'm comfortable on both sides of the chip boundary.

RISC-V RV32I Single-Cycle CPU

Verified

A complete single-cycle RV32I datapath (R/I-type, LW, SW, BEQ/BNE, JAL, JALR) built from 11 modules, each with its own directed self-checking testbench. Verified end to end with a hand-assembled integration program that exercises every instruction type, including branch and jump paths: 8/8 checks pass.

CNN Accelerator with RISC-V SoC Integration

WIP

Three-person team project: an MNIST digit classifier on a Cyclone IV FPGA, with a CNN accelerator (conv 3×3 → ReLU → max pool → dense → argmax) controlled by a PicoRV32 soft core. I own dataflow & memory: a BRAM line-buffer 3×3 sliding window with an AXI-Stream interface, safe under backpressure, verified with directed and constrained-random stall testbenches.

Parameterized Arithmetic IP Library

WIP

Configurable 8–128-bit integer arithmetic cores, each with one variant tuned for latency and one for logic cost:

  • Radix-4 Booth multiplier with a recursive Wallace (3:2) tree
  • Shift-and-add multiplier (cost-optimized)
  • Radix-2 SRT divider (verified at 8 and 128 bits)
  • Restoring divider (verified at 8 and 128 bits)
  • Parameterized N×N systolic matrix multiplier (verified at N = 2, 3)
Wider-width & larger-N verification next~80%
